Ruby CTF


In the context of cybersecurity, CTF (Capture The Flag) challenges are gamified competitions where participants, either individually or as teams, solve puzzles and tasks related to information security to find hidden "flags" or pieces of information, simulating real-world attack and defense scenarios.
In HTML-based Capture the Flag (CTF) challenges, flags are often hidden within web pages or related elements like: HTML Comments, Tags, Background, Hidden Elements, Encoded Content in the head or body.
